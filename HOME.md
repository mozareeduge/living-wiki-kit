## Authority rule (unchanged)

A canonical page is the reviewed current map of an object. It is not the
original. For a consequential statement:

1. open the canonical page;
2. inspect its claim and relation records;
3. open source records;
4. use derivatives to locate passages;
5. open the immutable original when exact form matters.
