## A. Landscape map (grouped by approach)

### A1. Design research axis (Frayling → RtD)
- **Frayling (1993), "Research in Art and Design"** (RCA, grey literature): the root of the *into / through / about* triad. NO DOI registration [S01][S12]. The triad is witnessed through DOI-bearing secondary literature (Frankel & Racine, DRS, DOI 10.21606/drs.2008.43, 123 cites) [S13].
- **Zimmerman, Forlizzi & Evenson (2007), CHI**: "research through design" formalized for HCI — designers produce artifacts that "transform the world from its current state to a preferred state", contributions evaluated through four lenses, addressing under-constrained problems. DOI 10.1145/1240624.1240704, **2,275 citations** — the highest-velocity anchor in the whole family [S02].

### A2. Creative-arts / practice axis
- **Practice-based research** (Candy tradition): creative practice as a *method of inquiry* whose outcomes are new knowledge communicable in rich, scrutinizable forms — Candy & Edmonds 2017, Leonardo, DOI 10.1162/leon_a_01471, 313 cites [S03].
- **Practice-led vs research-led** (Smith & Dean 2009, EUP, DOI 10.1515/9780748636303, 553 cites): practice-led research = practice generates insights; research-led practice = research feeds back into practice; the relation is "iterative and web-like" [S04]. This bidirectionality is the sharpest available external gloss on "research through practice".
- **Practice as Research / PaR** (Nelson 2013, Palgrave, DOI 10.1057/9781137282910, 371 cites; Barrett & Bolt 2014, 494 cites, no DOI; Sullivan 2005, 729 cites, review-DOI caveat): performance/visual-arts institutional strand with explicit principles, protocols, "know-how/know-that" blending [S05][S07][S08].

### A3. Artistic research axis (Continental/institutional)
- **Borgdorff (2012), The Conflict of the Faculties** (Leiden UP, open access): "Artistic research is an endeavour in which the artistic and the academic are connected… artistic practices contribute as research to what we know and understand." DOI 10.26530/oapen_595042, 370 cites [S06]. Artistic research = the *institutional-epistemological* program: art practice admitted as knowledge production inside academia, with its own exposition culture (cf. T6: Research Catalogue, JAR, SAR).

### A4. Poetry axis (the user's "research poetry")
- **Poetic inquiry** (Prendergast, Leggo & Sameshima 2009 handbook, DOI 10.1163/9789087909512, 296 cites) [S09]: poetry as qualitative-research methodology.
- **Research poetry** — Lahman et al. 2011, Qualitative Inquiry, DOI 10.1177/1077800411423219 [S10]; Davis 2019 "critical poetic inquiry", DOI 10.1177/1077800419884978 [S11]: the canonical *external* sense is poem as **representation of qualitative data** (poems composed from interview corpora).

**Decisive contrast for the archive:** in the external literature, "research poetry" is *data → poem* (representation). In Mozare's practice, research poetry is *source-field → poem* where the poem is itself an **instrument of inquiry** (speculative research poetry, شعر پژوهشی گمانه‌زن): it stages uncertain relations while their status stays available — closer to the practice-based/artistic-research family than to Lahman's representational sense, while remaining formally distinct from both.
