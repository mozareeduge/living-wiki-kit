## A. Landscape map
(grouped: core theory · computational/digital/media applications · curatorial/artistic uptake · criticism)

### A1. Core theory & founding positions

- **Genealogy.** Speculative realism names itself at the Goldsmiths College workshop, April 2007 (Toscano moderating; Brassier, Grant, Harman, Meillassoux), with post-Kantian "correlationism" as the shared target [S8]. Brassier publicly dissolved the label in 2011 ("orgy of stupidity"), rejecting the blog-mediated movement culture in which OOO grew [S9]; the Wikipedia article itself carries an original-research banner as of May 2025 [S10]. Harman's own blog states the lineage formula: classical metaphysics mated with actor-network theory [S7].
- **Founding texts and footprints.** Bryant, *Democracy of Objects* (OHP, 2011, c=640) and Harman, *Realist Magic* (OHP, 2013, c=243) — OHP as open-access infrastructure of the movement [S3]. Bogost, *Alien Phenomenology, or What It's Like to Be a Thing* (UMP, c=1,192; Crossref-registered) [S26][S6]; Morton, *Hyperobjects* (UMP 2013, c=2,649 — the largest single footprint by far) [S23]. Harman's synthesis *OOO: A New Theory of Everything* (2018, c=622) leads philosophy-title counts [S22].
- **Institutional indexing.** PhilPapers maintains a dedicated OOO bibliography category (417 items, Aug 2026) — formal philosophical recognition, though first-page entries are manuscript-heavy [S14].

### A2. Computational/digital/media applications

- **Formal publication uptake is design/architecture-led.** Broader keyword sweep: OpenAlex records 3,936 works matching 'object-oriented ontology' from 2010 onward, rising monotonically from 11 (2010) to ~443/yr (2023–2025) [S1]. Of 255 title-level OOO works (narrower query, 2010+): "Design Research and Object-Oriented Ontology" (Open Philosophy 2020), "Digital Tool Thinking: OOO versus New Materialism" (ACADIA quarterly 2016 — computational architecture), "Turning Philosophy with a Speculative Lathe: OOO, carpentry" (DRS 2018) [S21].
- **No serious computational implementation exists.** GitHub holds only 6 repos matching "object-oriented ontology", all personal/experimental toys (≤4 stars); arXiv has zero relevant entries [S19][S20]. The phrase's collision with object-oriented programming has produced database/engineering papers that are unrelated to the philosophy (e.g., OWL-in-OODB storage work appearing under the same title query) [S21].
- **Hyperobjects as infrastructure for Anthropocene/platform critique.** Citing works cluster in climate communication, education, archaeology, platform studies [S5][S23], with a dedicated peer-reviewed critique of the concept's scientific usefulness in WIREs Climate Change (2016) [S24].
- **Term divergence risk: 'ontography'.** Bogost's ontography competes with a laboratory-studies 'ontography' (Social Studies of Science 2013, c=197) and media-theory uses (ZfMK 2019) [S25][S4].

### A3. Curatorial/artistic uptake
- **Publication uptake is design-led, not museum-led.** The strongest art/design-flavored titles in the OpenAlex top-cited OOO corpus are more-than-human design and media-ontology works — e.g., Bryant's *Onto-Cartography: An Ontology of Machines and Media*, "More-Than Human Centred Design", "Decentering Through Design" [S2][S21]. No flagship museum exhibition catalogue surfaced in any query.
- **OOO's art-world channel runs through theory publishers and blogs**, not institutions: OHP's open-access list [S3][S12], Harman's blog as announcement organ [S7], and Bogost's *Alien Phenomenology* (c=1,192) as the practice-oriented bridge text whose 'ontography'/'carpentry' vocabulary artists cite [S26]. 
- **Assessment (candidate-tier):** compared with e.g. speculative realism's gallery visibility claims in secondary discourse, verifiable institutional curatorial uptake of OOO is thin; the movement's infrastructure is publishing + blogs. This matters for the wiki: its 'curatorial practice context' has little external OOO-curatorial literature to bind to.

### A4. Criticism & counter-pressure
- **Indigenous/feminist critique of the 'ontological turn'.** Zoe Todd, "An Indigenous Feminist's Take On The Ontological Turn: 'Ontology' Is Just Another Word For Colonialism" (J Historical Sociology 2016, c=1,813; count qualified by author-disambiguation noise in raw name searches [S18]) — the single highest-cited critical intervention captured; argues ontological anthropology/OOO-adjacent turns recolonize Indigenous thought by rebranding it [S27][S13].
- **Systematic philosophical critique.** Peter Wolfendale's *Object-Oriented Philosophy: The Noumenon's New Clothes* (deontologistics.co hub) is the most sustained internal-camp critique (correlationism charge against Harman); the blog remains active through 2025, now applying post-Searlean critique frames to LLMs [S16][S28].
- **Movement-skepticism from within.** Sparrow: SR/OOO is a style, not a movement; Brassier: a movement cannot be bound merely to anti-correlationism; Brassier publicly disowned the label entirely [S9][S29].
- **Terence Blake's review-line** charges Harman with recurring subjectivism ('Materialism without matter...') [S15][S22].
- **Hyperobjects under empirical pressure:** WIREs Climate Change (2016) critiques the concept's scientific usefulness [S24].
- **Term-collision pressure:** 'ontography' is polysemic across STS/media theory [S25]; 'object-oriented ontology' itself collides with object-oriented programming in database literatures [S21].
