# Purpose

Product and strategy work where decisions must stay revisable as evidence arrives.

# What this profile helps the system notice

Why a decision was made, what would reopen it, and which signals are still
unverified.

# Anti-patterns

Do not record decisions without their reasons; a decision without provenance
is a rumor.
