# Speculative research poetry is formally distinct from external research poetry

## Current statement

In the external literature, 'research poetry' is data-to-poem (representation of qualitative data; Lahman et al. 2011). In the operator's practice, research poetry is source-field to poem where the poem is itself an instrument of inquiry (speculative research poetry, شعر پژوهشی گمانه‌زن) — closer to the practice-based/artistic-research family while formally distinct from both. 'Research poetry' is the author's own term (author confirmation 2026-09-06).
